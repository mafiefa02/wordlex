Open Graph cards — four takes

The 1200×630 image a link to wordlex.afiefabd.com unfurls with, in Slack, WhatsApp, Discord, iMessage and the rest. Each card is the real size, shown at half; the small one beside it is the same card at the size a chat client actually renders a thumbnail. The site has none today, so a shared link is text on a blank rectangle.

All four pin a dark palette and use CONTEXT.md's words. Whichever wins gets built as src/app/opengraph-image.tsx, which draws through Satori — flexbox only, no CSS grid — so every layout here is expressible in what that renderer accepts.

A — Hero

The landing page's own headline, at the landing page's own weight. Someone who follows the link lands on the sentence they just read. Nothing but type and the mark, which is the only take that survives being resized by a client that ignores the aspect ratio.

B — Board

A part-played board beside the headline, Marks only, no letters — the same rule the Story card holds. It says "word game" before a word of the copy is read, which is the one thing type alone cannot do.

C — Twelve

The twelve Tracks as a wall, each in its language hue. The breadth is the pitch, so this take makes the breadth the picture. The busiest of the three, and the one that dies at thumbnail size.

D — Band

The board across the full width, with the headline above it and the promise below. Seven Tiles rather than five, because the headline says "more than five letters" and this is the one take with room to show it. The board stops being an illustration beside the copy and becomes the thing the card is.
